Conclusion: hospitals row id=1 can only ever be modified by one transaction at a time. Both fixes are
correct and worth keeping (removed real, unnecessary latency), but the remaining ~8/s ceiling is
structural — a single hot row's contention limit, not a bug fixable further inside this transaction.
Real fix for the ticket's full mass-casualty scenario needs an architectural change (queue, sharded
counters, or eventual consistency) outside the scope of this transaction.
