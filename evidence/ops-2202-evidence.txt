RESULT SUMMARY — successful throughput actually DROPPED under this attempt (retry-storm effect)
=====================================================================
                          | Attempt 1 (pool=20, unbounded queue) | Attempt 2 (pool=20, queueLimit=50)
Successful requests       | 30,019                                | 8,502
Successful rate           | ~858/s                                 | ~243/s
Error rate                | 2.63%                                  | 86.86% (mostly clean, fast rejections)
Median (successful)       | 896.79ms                               | 204.48ms

Explanation: k6 script has no backoff — a rejected VU immediately retries in a tight loop. Once rejections
became fast (~100ms) instead of hanging (10-30s), total http_reqs roughly doubled (30,831 -> 64,733) without
a corresponding rise in genuine successful throughput — a self-inflicted retry storm against a system still
genuinely over capacity. The fix (bounded queue) is correct; full benefit needs client-side backoff too.
